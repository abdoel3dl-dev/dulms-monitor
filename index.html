DULMS Monitor

A personal academic assistant for Delta University LMS.

DULMS Monitor checks the student portal on a schedule, reports new assignments, quizzes, exams, grades and absences, and mirrors deadlines to Google Calendar. It sends reminders only to its owner.

What it does

  • Tracks deadlines and quiz windows from DULMS.
  • Creates and updates calendar events with reminders.
  • Sends optional WhatsApp notifications to the account owner.
  • Stores synchronization state in a private GitHub repository.

Who it is for

This is a single-user, personal-use integration. It is not a public service and does not offer accounts or collect data from other people.
